## Trust is about behaviour under pressure

A chatbot that gives a wrong answer wastes a reader's time. An agent that takes a wrong action can spend money, send a message or delete a file. The argument of this piece is that trust in agents should rest less on how often they are right and more on how they fail.

## Four properties worth asking for

1. **Visible scope.** People can see what the agent may and may not do before it starts.
2. **Enforced limits.** Those limits are enforced outside the model, not only stated in its instructions.
3. **Checkpoints.** Irreversible actions pause for confirmation.
4. **A record.** Every action can be inspected afterwards.
